DoingRightNow.com

A journal for getting unstuck

Don’t plan your entire day.
Just start with what’s right in front of you.

Not a planner, to-do list, or habit tracker. Write one timestamped line — what you just did, or the tiny next step — and do that.

Start Doing Right Now

No sign-up required. Stays on this device.

Each line lowers the bar for the next one. If you drift, the last line is exactly where you left off.

Shrink the next step

Don’t write “Write the report.” Write “Open the document.” Tiny starts beat big plans.

Empty your working memory

Your brain is a processor, not a storage drive. Log the line so you don’t have to hold it in your head.

Anchor your attention

Your timeline is a safety net. Come back anytime and see exactly what you were just doing.

One prompt: What are you doing right now? · Today, 2d, 3d, 7d, All · No accounts, no cloud, no tracking

Why a single line can help

There’s research on this, not just a productivity slogan. Getting a task out of your head, shrinking it until you can start, and noticing what you just did are well-studied ways people get unstuck. This journal is simply a place to do those three things.

If you want them

Search when you need to look back, and export just those matches. A full copy is always in Settings. Notes is a scratchpad; Quick Add saves lines you reuse. Any past entry can be brought into Today — the original stays where it was.

You can also tag a line with @person, #location, or *project — a ! makes it stand out.
