## Short (~80 characters)

A journal for getting unstuck — one timestamped line at a time. No sign-up, no cloud.

## Medium (~250 characters)

Doing Right Now is a minimalist journal for task paralysis. Don't plan your day — write one line for what you're doing right now, timestamp it, and move on. When it changes, write the next line. Not a planner or to-do list. Free, private, on your device only.

## Long (~500 characters)

Doing Right Now (DRN) helps when a task feels too big to start. Instead of a backlog, you log one tiny, timestamped line: what you just did, or the smallest next step. Your last line always shows where you left off. Optional tags, search, export, and Add to Home Screen — but no accounts, no cloud sync, no streaks, no pressure to finish. A spark for momentum, not a replacement for the planner you already use.
