Don’t plan your entire day. Just start with what’s right in front of you.

A journal built for getting unstuck. When a task feels too big to start, don’t plan it — just write down the tiny next step and do that. Jot the time and one line: what you just did, or the small thing you’re about to do. Each entry lowers the bar to starting the next one, and your last line always shows exactly where you left off if you drift and come back. No planning, no streaks, no pressure to finish — just a trail of small starts that add up.

What you gotta know

Some things are tucked away on purpose. Once you find them, they stay out of the way until you need them.

  • Type a line under Today and press Enter.
  • Tap a line to edit the words. Very long lines truncate — tap once to expand, tap again to edit. You can’t change the time or reorder the day.
  • Swipe left to reveal delete, then tap. On a computer, hover the line and use the trash icon.
  • Swipe right to reveal copy to Today, then tap. The original stays. On a computer, hover and use the calendar icon.
  • Search is in the Today header. Two tags find either one; quote them to require both. gym finds the word; #gym finds tagged lines only. Search xx for completed lines. Use the In: buttons (Today, 2d, 7d, All) — pick All for your full journal.
  • View Today, 2d, 3d, 7d, or All. Export uses the same ranges — tap one below View. Exports are backup only; you can’t import them back.
  • Theme (above Settings): light or dark, then pick an accent from the swatches. Green is the default.
  • Settings has font size, trophy threshold, and clear options. Optional: Timer on current line shows elapsed time on today’s latest entry — a nudge, not a timesheet.
  • On a past day, click the date to rate it with stars.
  • Your journal lives in this browser on this device — no cloud. Phone and laptop are separate; only tabs in the same browser stay in sync. Export All for backup. Clearing browser data can wipe it too.
  • On a phone, Add to Home Screen for an app icon.
Optional: @person · #location · *project · any ! makes the line bold and red · xx as its own word strikes it out.

Calling all clowns 🤡

Search is plain text — a single emoji works on any device. Type or paste one character to find every line that used it.

  • 🤡 — mark a distraction or rabbit hole; it jumps out when you scroll back
  • ✅ — tag a small win; one tap from the emoji keyboard, easy to spot in the feed

Notes and Quick Add

Notes

Some things don’t belong on a timestamped line — a URL, a thought for after this, a reminder before bed. Notes is a scratchpad for that. It sits under Today and auto-saves.

Notes is one overall pad, not a note for each day. You won’t see Notes under Monday or Tuesday. Export All puts Notes once at the bottom, not beside each day. Search export leaves Notes out.

Don’t turn it into a second planner. You can ignore Notes. The journal still works.

You can’t change a time or reorder the day. You can edit the words of any line — handy if you later add @Bill or #home for search.

Quick Add

If you type the same line often, save it once. Open Quick Add, type the line, press Enter. Tap it later to log it now, with a new timestamp. Tags work here too.

  • open the document *Report
  • walk #park
  • email @Bill
  • standup *Website
