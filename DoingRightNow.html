Don’t plan your entire day. Just start with what’s right in front of you.

A journal built for getting unstuck. When a task feels too big to start, don’t plan it — just write down the tiny next step and do that. Jot the time and one line: what you just did, or the small thing you’re about to do. Each entry lowers the bar to starting the next one, and your last line always shows exactly where you left off if you drift and come back. No planning, no streaks, no pressure to finish — just a trail of small starts that add up.

💡 Tip: Swipe left to delete · Swipe right to bring an entry into Today (keeps the original). On desktop, hover an entry for action buttons.
Optional: @person · #location · *project · any ! makes the line bold and red · xx strikes it out.
