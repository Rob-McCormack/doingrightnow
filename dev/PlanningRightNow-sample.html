This is a sample

A sketch of the sister app to Doing Right Now. Same shell. Different job. Not the live plugin or website.

The job

Doing Right Now answers what am I doing right now. Planning Right Now answers what is ahead of me. It looks forward. There is no Yesterday. A checkbox is the whole verb.

Quick start

  • Write one task in Today, Tomorrow, This Week, or Sometime.
  • Check it off. It goes to Done.
  • Uncheck it on Done to bring it back to Today.
  • Type @ people, + projects, = places. MUSTDO! pins a line at the top. ! marks urgent. 20% shows progress.

Not in this sample

Horizon notes, Archive, reports, milestones, and the Obsidian plugin. Those stay in the real Planning Right Now if we keep them.
